# UDA-Hub Knowledge Base

## Refunds
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase if the plan is eligible and no policy abuse is detected. Standard refunds can be processed automatically for verified orders under five hundred dollars.

## Password Reset
For account access issues, ask the customer to use the password reset link. If multi-factor authentication is unavailable or the user cannot access their email, escalate to identity verification.

## Billing Invoice
Invoices can be resent to the billing email on file. For address changes or tax corrections, route to billing support.

## Technical Outage
If a customer reports a production outage, collect affected workspace, timestamps, error messages, and urgency. Escalate high severity issues immediately.

## Account Cancellation
Cancellation requests should include account id, reason, and confirmation that the customer understands data retention policy.
